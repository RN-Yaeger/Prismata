Privacy

Short version: no cookies, no ads, no accounts, no personal profiles. Anonymous visitor and download numbers are counted with GoatCounter.

What this site is

PRISMATA is a free, non-commercial fan project. It offers themes for the Lian Li 8.8" Universal Screen as downloads. Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising.

Hosting by GitHub Pages

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page or download a file, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, such as your IP address, the time of the request and your browser type. GitHub needs this to deliver the site and to keep its service secure. The site owner has no access to these logs. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Statistics with GoatCounter

To see how many people visit and which themes are downloaded, this site uses GoatCounter, an open-source, privacy-friendly statistics service. A small script is loaded from GoatCounter's server (gc.zgo.at). It counts page views and download clicks and records the page, the referring site (e.g. Discord), screen size, browser, operating system and country.

GoatCounter sets no cookies, stores no IP addresses and builds no profiles. Visits cannot be linked to you or followed across other websites. Only the site owner sees the totals. Details: GoatCounter privacy policy.

Fonts and images are loaded from this site itself, not from third parties.

Your saved choices

Your last setup (screen, clock, pump) is stored in your own browser (local storage), so the page remembers it next time. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Contact

Questions about this site: open an issue on the project's GitHub page, github.com/RN-Yaeger/Prismata.
