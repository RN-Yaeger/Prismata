Install in under a minute

Works with L-Connect 3 and the Lian Li 8.8" Universal Screen.

  1. Download

    Choose screen, clock and pump above, then hit Download on the colour you like.

  2. Keep it zipped

    Don't unzip the file. L-Connect imports the .zip as it is.

  3. Import

    Open L-Connect 3, go to the Universal Screen and click the "+" tile. Select the .zip, done.

Pump: "With pump" shows your AIO pump RPM. No compatible AIO? Use "No pump".

Font: uses Microsoft YaHei, which ships with Windows. Nothing to install.

RAM total shows "--"? Then L-Connect isn't reporting it on your system.

Bright themes: Stormtrooper and Mono Light have a white background.
