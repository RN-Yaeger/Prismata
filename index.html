Welcome to PRISMATA.
Themes for your 8.8" screen.

PRISMATA is a free, fan-made collection of sensor themes for the Lian Li 8.8" Universal Screen. Every theme shows your live CPU, GPU and system stats in L-Connect 3. There are two designs, each in 30 colourways, horizontal or vertical, 24h or 12h clock, with or without pump RPM.

How it works

  1. Pick a design

    PRISMATA 2.0 or PRISMATA HUD. Open its page below.

  2. Choose your setup and colour

    Screen, clock and pump, then one of 30 colourways. You get one .zip.

  3. Import in L-Connect 3

    Keep it zipped. Universal Screen, click the "+" tile, select the .zip, done.
